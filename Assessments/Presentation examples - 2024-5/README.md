# Presentation examples - 2024-5

This folder includes real-life slide decks from 2024-5. When you're looking at the slides, remember that their product demos are not included, as they will have shown the live system to us outside of the slides.  The best presentations had really strong demos as the centre of their presentations. 

**GitHub will only show you a certain number of slides, so you might want to download these to make sure you see the full presentations!**

The MVP slides are obviously aimed at that stage, but the structure and level of information is really strong, and will be useful to look at for beta and final.  

**VR Voice Coaching** MVP slides are included because they are an excellent example of a team understanding where they've been going wrong and having a solid plan to improve.
