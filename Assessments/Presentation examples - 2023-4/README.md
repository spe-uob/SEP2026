# Presentation examples

This folder includes real-life slide decks from 2023. Please don't just read the MVP/Beta slides - look at all 3 from any team to see how they iterated and refined their slides over the course of the year. 

For example, some of the examples of user stories start of with lots of words in the MVP slides, but by the end are presented very differently.  

When you're looking at the slides, remember that their product demos are not included, as they will have shown the live system to us outside of the slides.
