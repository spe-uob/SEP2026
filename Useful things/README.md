## Spaces to meet your team

There are lots of spaces you can book around the University. Sometimes they are limited to 1 or 2 hour slots - but of course, if 1 person in your group books 9-11, and the next 11-12, you have 3 hours! 

* **[Queen's Building study spaces](https://outlook.office.com/book/ENGFStudentStudyRoomsBookingSystem@bristol.ac.uk/?ae=true&login_hint)**: they have whiteboards and screens; the ones on the 1st floor are much quieter.  Click on a room, click on a dark date, then for a specific room, click 'select staff' drop down.

* **[Study rooms across campus](https://bristol.ac.uk/library/use/study-seat-booking/)**:  You can book rooms in a bunch of the libraries and study centres like Beacon House and Senate House - booking for individual seats and rooms [here](https://librarybookings.bristol.ac.uk/)  

* **CS student lab**: MVB 2.56 is a lab space just for you - in the MVB atrium, go all the way past the rainbow benches, turning right by the yellow wall, past the bathrooms and left all the way down a long corridor.  Bear in mind in TB2 the Y3 MEng Team Project students have priority, so if it gets busy you may be asked to leave. 

* **Non-bookable study spaces** like MVB rainbow benches, and [Brambles](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/students/your-studies/libraries-study-spaces/study-spaces/brambles-study-centre/), [Senate House](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/students/your-studies/libraries-study-spaces/study-spaces/senate-house/) and [Beacon House](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/students/your-studies/libraries-study-spaces/study-spaces/beacon-house/) - these tend to fill up fast, but if you have someone who liked mornings in your group, get them to come in and stake out your favourite MVB booth!

## Spaces to meet your client on campus

If you want to meet your client on campus, or book a space to videocall them, you can book a **[Queen's Building study spaces](https://outlook.office.com/book/ENGFStudentStudyRoomsBookingSystem@bristol.ac.uk/?ae=true&login_hint)** (the ones on the 1st floor are quieter!) or a **[Study room on campus](https://bristol.ac.uk/library/use/study-seat-booking/)**.   Click on a room, click on a dark date, then for a specific room, click 'select staff' drop down.. Please try those first!  But if they're not available, send Sarah Connolly a Teams message at least 3 working days in advance with the date, time, and how many people it's for, and we can try to book you a meeting room in MVB or Queens. 

**If you meet your client on campus**, don't forget how hard it is to navigate our buildings!  Meet them at the nearest entrance and take them to the room yourself - and don't forget to tell them how to recognise you.

### AWS cloud account 
Request it from [Technical Services Cloud page](https://www.ole.bris.ac.uk/webapps/blackboard/content/listContentEditable.jsp?content_id=_9948566_1&course_id=_238502_1) (you must be logged into Blackboard)

> * Please login to blackboard on the device you are using.
> * Follow this link  https://tinyurl.com/BB-TechnicalServices to the Technical Services blackboard site. > > Don't worry if you see an access denied message, just continue to the next step.
> 
> * If this is the first time accessing our blackboard site, you will need to enrol using the green `+Enrol` button on the left-hand menu and submit the form.

**Before you make the cloud request** please complete the cloud induction as a team, so you all understand your responsibilities as a Cloud user, and understand what you need to do.  

Remember to put in the unit code COMS20006. In the reason you want it, say something like:
 
> *I am a student on COMS20006 Software Engineering Project, and my team are making [eg a web app to do X].  My Unit Director, Sarah Connolly, has said that we need AWS Cloud for this and asked that I request it.*
>
> *The team members using this account will be:*
> 
>*[name] [username]*
>
>*Thanks very much*

Where it asks you to input supervisor details, put my name - Sarah Connolly - and my email sarah.connolly@bristol.ac.uk
 
**Please read the [Workbook on Cloud](https://github.com/spe-uob/SEP2025/tree/main/Weekly%20Workbooks/07%20Containers%20and%20Cloud) before you start using it**.

**Your first Cloud task** is to add all the team-mates as administrators, so if the person who set it up gets ill, or goes on holiday, the rest of the team can access the account.

## Support with research
Our [Computer Science Librarian](https://bristol.libguides.com/computer-science) can help you with any research questions and will even find you papers and articles for you. She has access to all the other University librarians, so if you want to explore best practice on User Interfaces, issues around accessibility, successful ways to do specific tasks (eg to help people learn) she is a fantastic resource. 

Go to the [Computer Science Library Guide](https://bristol.libguides.com/computer-science) and you can make an appointment with her to tell her what you're looking for. 

If you've never read an academic paper before, there is a short blog about it [here](https://studyskills.blogs.bristol.ac.uk/2022/11/07/student-perspective-how-to-read-academic-articles-without-getting-overwhelmed/).
